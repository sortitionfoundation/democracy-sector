_To ensure recommendations are acted on and embedded into decision making._

Links to Principles: [A Clear Mandate](/principles/#a-clear-mandate)

## Core

_The non-negotiable minimum_

Commission signed off by relevant organisational senior leadership, political leads and/or decision makers, as relevant. Clear routes for keeping internal leadership updated on progress and outcomes, with an agreed route for response.

## Desirable

_Extra depth where resources allow_

Stakeholder mapping within the organisation – covering both those the process depends on (e.g. political sponsors or organisational leads) and those it's meant to influence (e.g. policymakers and decision makers) – with relevant colleagues informed and engaged. Clear routes for keeping internal colleagues updated and involved in the response and succession planning for staff changes.

## Stretch

_Context-specific, deeper power sharing, sustaining and embedding_

External stakeholder mapping beyond the organisation, identifying those with key links to the process question; work undertaken to ensure they know about the process and are on board. Clear routes for keeping external stakeholders updated on progress and outcomes, and involved in response as appropriate.
