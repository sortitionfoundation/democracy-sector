_So all voices are heard, not just the loudest or most powerful._

Links to Principles: [Well-designed Deliberation](/principles/#well-designed-deliberation)

## Core

_The non-negotiable minimum_

Impartial, skilled facilitation, with clear protocols for how facilitators, speakers and observers interact with participants. Facilitators actively probe for underlying values and concerns beyond participants' initial positions, not only managing discussion flow, and support all voices to be heard through actively managing power dynamics. Design reviewed by an independent advisory group.

## Desirable

_Extra depth where resources allow_

Delivery body independent of the commissioner. Facilitator teams are diverse – for example in age, gender, ethnicity and class.

## Stretch

_Context-specific, deeper power sharing, sustaining and embedding_

Facilitators trained or supported by those with trauma-informed, power-aware practice.
