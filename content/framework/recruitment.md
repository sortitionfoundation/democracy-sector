_So the process includes the right people to deliberate on the question — whether that means reflecting the diversity of the wider population, or bringing together people with direct experience of the issue._

Links to Principles: [Inclusion-focused](/principles/#inclusion-focused)

## Core

_The non-negotiable minimum_

A recruitment method that's transparent about who it's trying to reach and why, gives people a fair chance of being selected, and pays attention to diversity and reflectiveness - making it difficult for interest groups or self-selecting individuals to secure a place. Who's recruited may be drawn from the wider population, or from people with direct lived experience of the issue at hand, depending on what the process needs. (For example the [Minimum Income Guarantee experts by experience panel](https://www.gov.scot/publications/minimum-income-guarantee-experts-experience-panel-report/).) Either way, selection should be random wherever possible, with complementary, targeted recruitment — for example to reach marginalised or less-responsive groups — not ruled out.

## Desirable

_Extra depth where resources allow_

Recruitment methodology is actively reviewed for inclusivity, with realistic attention to response rates among underrepresented groups.

## Stretch

_Context-specific, deeper power sharing, sustaining and embedding_

The process actively works to reduce structural inequality through who is recruited — for example ensuring strong representation of most-affected groups, or partnering with trusted community organisations to reach people less likely to respond to standard recruitment.
