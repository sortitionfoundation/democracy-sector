## Contributors

These standards were developed through roundtables and workshops with four key groups - deliberative practitioners, commissioners of deliberative processes, independent evaluators, and past participants in deliberative processes. We would like to extend our sincere thanks to everyone who gave their time and insights to these roundtables.

The framework has been shaped by members of the Deliberation Network Standards Group, who have led the synthesis and development work throughout. The group includes independent practitioners as well as representatives of organisations. The organisations that have been involved throughout the process are: [Citizens in Power](https://www.citizensinpower.com/), [Involve](https://www.involve.org.uk/), [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-uk), [Mutual Gain](https://www.mutualgain.org/), the [National Centre for Social Research](https://natcen.ac.uk/), [Shared Future](https://sharedfuturecic.org.uk/), and the [Sortition Foundation](https://www.sortitionfoundation.org/).

## Feedback

These standards are not set in stone. What matters most is that they are used in practice and work for different audiences. We want to hear from practitioners, commissioners, delivery organisations, evaluators, researchers and more about what needs to change to make the standards stronger and more applicable to real-world deliberative practice.

We welcome your feedback and suggestions. Please share them with us via [our Google Form](https://forms.gle/PKTxsqgnrVRi9dYK7).
