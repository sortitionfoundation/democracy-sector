Deliberative public engagement, done well, has the potential to help resolve some of our most intractable collective challenges. By bringing the insights and lived experience of a diverse group of the public to bear on critical issues we can reach better decisions that have broader support – while reducing polarisation, increasing people's agency, and revitalising our democracy. Realising that potential depends on deliberative engagement being carried out with integrity and earning the genuine trust of the public and decision makers. This framework sets out four good practice principles that underpin the 11 standards for deliberative engagement:

## A Clear Mandate

A clear purpose for the process, with real buy-in and accountability so recommendations are taken forward and meaningfully inform decisions, within conditions that make it possible for power to be genuinely shared.

Links to Standards: [1. Genuine Purpose](/framework/genuine-purpose/), [2. Accountability](/framework/accountability/), [3. Buy-in](/framework/buy-in/)

## Inclusion-focused

The process reflects the population it speaks for and actively considers at all stages - from recruitment and participant support, to framing and information giving - how to be genuinely accessible to, and inclusive of, lesser heard voices.

Links to Standards: [4. Recruitment](/framework/recruitment/), [5. Inclusion & Accessibility](/framework/inclusion-and-accessibility/)

## Well-designed Deliberation

A well-designed process that supports a genuine journey of learning, deliberation and considered judgement for participants.

Links to Standards: [6. Balanced Information](/framework/balanced-information/), [7. Impartial Facilitation](/framework/impartial-facilitation/), [8. Time for Learning and Deliberation](/framework/time-for-learning-and-deliberation/), [9. Considered Judgement](/framework/considered-judgement/)

## Trust & Learning

A transparent process, with an evaluation that captures learning to improve impact and future practice.

Links to Standards: [10. Transparency](/framework/transparency/), [11. Integrity, Impact & Evaluation](/framework/integrity-impact-and-evaluation/)
